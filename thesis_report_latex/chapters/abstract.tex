It is now expected that most university teachers create interactive learning materials, and
widely-used authoring systems expect some degree of technical expertise that a working
teacher may lack. Even preparing a simple three-question H5P interactive video in WordPress
can take over 45~minutes at Vietnam National University~-- Ho Chi Minh City (VNU-HCM),
making it difficult to incorporate interactive content into regular course preparation. To
close this gap, this thesis introduces an H5P interactive video content creation
platform designed to make authoring easier while keeping instructors in control of the
content. The proposed system uses the following components:
a front-end in React~18 and TypeScript, a back-end in Node.js/Express, and a SQLite
database. At its heart, it is an AI pipeline capable of creating candidate H5P interactions
based on uploaded caption files, YouTube transcripts, or audio recordings. Every item
created is then validated in the pipeline before being displayed to instructors, enabling a
human-in-the-loop process that promotes transparency and usability. The platform is
designed to reduce the amount of AI processing knowledge that teachers must possess.

A comparative usability evaluation was carried out with 10 students of information
technology against an H5P authoring workflow in WordPress. The results show significant
improvements in learning-content production efficiency and effectiveness: task completion
climbed from 30\% to 95\%, and the median time to manually create five interactions was
reduced from 18~minutes to 5~minutes. The System Usability Scale (SUS) score rose from 38.0 to
82.5, indicating that the usability of the system improved from ``Poor'' to ``Excellent'' on
Brooke's scale. Overall, the results indicate that, with appropriate constraints and a
user-centred design approach, generative AI can make interactive video authoring accessible
to teachers who might otherwise be discouraged by the technical complexity.
